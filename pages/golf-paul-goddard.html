How your plan worksRound days, range practice and how every shot is scored

Two rounds a week with both warm-ups, plus range practice. Every scored shot counts against a scratch golfer.

Round day warm-up

  1. Pick putting first or range first.
  2. Putting warm-up (15 mins): 5 min start-line gate, a short Hole Out (12 putts from 3, 4 and 5 ft) and a short Money Stealers (3 stations).
  3. Range warm-up (35 mins): the Practice Plan 30 min approach and driver session (body warm up, ladder to 70 yds, strike station, 8 drives, 8 approaches, warm down) plus your 5 min technical station.

Range practice

  1. The Practice Plan warm-up, your technical station, then Wedge Darts: 18 balls with strokes gained on every shot.

Scoring

  • Enter carry and offline from the launch monitor after every full shot.
  • Every scored shot shows its strokes gained against scratch. Plus is better than scratch, minus is worse.
  • Every session gets a handicap: the level whose average golfer would have matched you exactly.
  • Your bests are kept under My PBs. At the end of the week, tap Send my week to Jack.
