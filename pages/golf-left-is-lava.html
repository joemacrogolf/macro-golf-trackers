How to playThe aim, the setup and how every ball is scored

Hit the green and keep the ball out of the lava. A miss to the safe side costs nothing; a big miss to the lava side costs 2.

Setup

  1. Set the three distances in Settings (the player's weakest approach yardages). Default is 115, 135 and 165 yds.
  2. Set a green with a pin at each distance on the sim.
  3. Enter the player's name, pick a target handicap and choose the lava side (left by default).

The game

  1. 18 balls: 6 at each distance, called in a random order on screen.
  2. Full pre-shot routine. Call the flight before every ball: stock flight unless the screen calls a shift.
  3. After each ball, enter carry and offline from the sim, tap which side of the pin it finished, then tap Score ball.

Handicap guide

How these numbers were worked out

Each par is the expected score over a full 18-ball game at the three distances set below, so it changes if you change the distances.

The starting point is average approach proximity from the fairway. PGA Tour players finish roughly 20 ft away from 115 yds and 28 ft from 165 yds. Scratch golfers finish about 35% further away, a 10 handicap about 90% further and a 20 handicap about 155% further.

On-course numbers include rough, slopes and awkward lies, so they were tightened by 30% for a perfect sim lie. Each shot was modelled with a carry miss that is 30% wider than the offline miss, plus a slight short bias that grows with handicap. Each shot was then scored with these exact rules.

These are estimates, not measured results. One game varies a lot, so judge the average over several sessions.

Rules
  • Set a green with a pin at each distance on the sim. 6 balls at each, 18 in total, called in a random order so the same distance never comes twice in a row.
  • Full pre-shot routine on every ball. Call the flight before you hit: stock flight unless the card calls a shift.
  • Enter carry and offline from the sim, and which side of the pin line the ball finished.
  • Proximity is worked out from the carry miss and the offline miss together.
  • Choose a target handicap at the top. Beat its par twice to unlock shifts: the last 6 balls are called as hold it straight or lower flight, scored the same way.
  • Lava side is left by default. Switch it to right for a player whose big miss goes right.
