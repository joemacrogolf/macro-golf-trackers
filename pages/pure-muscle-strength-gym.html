How to use

Read this before your first session

Pure Muscle & Strength builds the strength and physical capacity behind long-term golf performance: lower-body, pushing and pulling strength, muscle, hip and trunk strength, single-leg stability and rotational strength. The priorities are consistency, controlled technique and steady progress in your working weights.

  1. 1Pick your level, phase and week. If this type of training is new to you, start on Beginner. Train 2 or 3 times a week. On the 2-day option, do Day 1 and Day 2.
  2. 2Pick today's session and tick off the warm-up. Before your first heavy exercise, do 1–3 lighter preparation sets. Do not log those.
  3. 3Work through the blocks. Do A1, rest briefly, do A2, then rest the full time and repeat. Then move to B.
  4. 4After each set, enter the kg you lifted, your reps and your RPE. Tap the yellow suggestion to fill in the next load.
  5. 5Press Finish session. At the end of the week, send your report to your coach.
RPE 6
Moderate effort. About four reps left in the tank.
RPE 7
Challenging but comfortable. About three reps left.
RPE 8
Hard effort. About two reps left.
RPE 9
Very hard. About one rep left. Never go to failure.
Pyramids
Exercises shown as 12 / 10 / 8 get heavier each set: 12 reps at RPE 7, then add weight for 10 at RPE 8, then add weight again for 8 at RPE 9.
Load
The page uses your weight, reps and RPE to estimate your strength, then suggests the weight for your next set's target. If a set felt harder than planned, the next suggestion comes down. If it felt easier, it goes up. Your first set each session is suggested from your last session, so the weights rise as you get stronger. Dumbbell weights are per dumbbell.
Rest
Tap the Rest button on a block to start the countdown. It also starts by itself when you log the second exercise of a pair. Take longer on heavy lifts if you need it.
Technique
Lower the weight under control and lift with intent. Use the biggest comfortable range of motion, and avoid any position that causes pain.
Equipment
Dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, cable machines and general gym equipment.
Levels
Beginner: fewer sets and reps, with more rest. Intermediate: the standard programme. Advanced: an extra set on each exercise, with less rest. The exercises are the same on every level.
Saving
Your entries save on this device and browser. Use the same phone or computer each session to keep your history.
