How to playThe aim, the warm-up and how strikes work

Push your speed as high as it will go before you run out of strikes.

Setup

  1. Choose clubhead speed or ball speed.
  2. 5-minute body warm-up: get moving, activate the body, add some jumps.
  3. 5-minute hitting warm-up: start hitting balls and work into full shots.
  4. Hit 5 balls as fast as you can and enter each speed. Your fastest becomes the number to beat.

The game

  1. Every swing, enter the speed from the launch monitor.
  2. Faster than the number: it becomes the new number to beat.
  3. Slower than the number: that's a strike.
  4. Exactly the same: no change, no strike.
  5. 3 strikes and the game is over. Your score is the highest speed you reached.

At the end

  • Tap Send to coach to send a short text with your result.
  • Every session is saved, so you can track your personal best and the date you set it, and see this week against last week.
