Step 1 · Test

Find where you lose shots

Each test is scored in strokes gained against the handicap you're aiming for. Plus means you beat a golfer of that handicap from the same spots. Minus means you lose shots to them.

Long Game Test

Driving and approach play, with your real clubs and distances.

Short Game Test

Chipping, pitching and bunker play around the green.

Putting Test

Putts from every distance, valued by distance only. No guesswork about slope or break.

After six weeks you retest on exactly the same shots, so you can see what really moved.

Step 2 · My Numbers

Watch your game climb

Your strokes gained a round, area by area: driving, approach, short game and putting. It shows your biggest leak and your biggest strength.

  • Live: every game and plan session after a test moves your numbers.
  • Your trend over a month, three months, six months, a year or all time.
  • See exactly which shots the gains came from.

Step 3 · Your plan

A plan built from your tests

Your weekly Practice Plan puts most of your time where you lose the most shots. Each day you pick the session that suits you: range, short game or putting.

  • Every range session keeps your irons and driving sharp.
  • Miss one way? The plan adds drills that punish that side.
  • The warm-up is built in.
  • Games open from the plan and bring you straight back to it.
  • Working with a Macro Golf coach? Your 1-to-1 plan uses the same tools.

Understand it

What is strokes gained?

Strokes gained compares every shot with what a golfer of your target handicap would take from the same spot. It's the measure the tour uses, and it tells you where your shots really go. The app explains it in under a minute.
