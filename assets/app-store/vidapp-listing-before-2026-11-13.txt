App Store Description:
Macro Golf — Understand Your Game. Play Better Golf.

The Macro Golf App brings together the tools, training and resources we use to help golfers improve their performance.

Golf performance isn't determined by one thing. Your technique, physical capabilities, practice, strategy and mental performance all interact to influence the golf you produce.

Macro Golf helps you understand those different parts of your game and gives you practical ways to develop them.

Inside the Macro Golf App:

Golf Practice
Structured practice sessions and drills designed to help you develop your game and transfer improvements to the golf course.

Golf Fitness
Golf-specific strength, power, mobility and conditioning programmes, with exercise demonstrations and follow-along sessions.

Mobility & Physical Screening
Assess your movement and identify physical restrictions that may influence the movement solutions available to you.

Golf Swing & Technical Development
Drills and resources covering different areas of golf technique and movement.

Swing Speed Training
Structured training and exercises designed to help golfers develop clubhead speed and physical power.

Warm-Ups & Recovery
Golf-specific routines to prepare for practice and play, alongside mobility and recovery sessions.

Mental Performance
Resources designed to help you better understand the relationship between your thoughts, intentions, state and performance.

Education
Learn more about golf performance, practice, physical development and the principles behind the Macro Golf approach.

Whether you're working with the Macro Golf coaching team or using the app independently, the goal is the same: to help you better understand your game, develop your ability and access more of the golf you already possess.

Your swing. Your Blueprint. Your Match Up.
